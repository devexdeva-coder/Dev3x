Who's Building This

About

Divyansh Sharma is a student and independent builder. Dev3x is the technical identity that record lives under — an evolving one, not a finished brand. This site is a record of experiments, research directions, and things actually being built, across software, AI, cybersecurity, hardware, and eventually biology.

The interest isn't in using finished tools — it's in taking things apart to see how they work, then rebuilding them, then understanding why the rebuild broke. That's the pattern behind every project on this site, from an early robotics lab portal to a runtime security monitor to a filterless air purifier designed without lab access.
