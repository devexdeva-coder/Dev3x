Prompty

Plain text in, optimized prompt out. Paste a raw idea, answer a short Q&A, get back a structured, optimized AI prompt — powered by Groq.

StatusBeta
CategoryAI Tooling / UX
EngineGroq
CORE PROBLEM

Most people know roughly what they want from an AI system but not how to phrase it as an effective prompt. Prompty sits between the raw idea and the finished prompt.

HOW IT WORKS

A three-step flow: paste your idea → answer five quick multiple-choice questions so Prompty understands the actual task → get a generated, structured prompt with a prompt score attached, copyable in one click.

WHAT I LEARNED

Building the Q&A step taught more about UX than about prompting itself — the hard part wasn't generating a good prompt, it was asking the right five questions to extract enough context from someone who hasn't fully articulated what they want yet.

NEXT STEP

Expanding past the fixed five-question flow toward something that adapts its questions to the type of idea it's given.
